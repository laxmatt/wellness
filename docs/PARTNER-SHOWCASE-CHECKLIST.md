# Partner showcase checklist

What the repository holds on each of the 20 products, as a partner would
find it: the facts, the price evidence and the images. Generated by
`scripts/partner-checklist.ts`, so every figure comes from the catalogue
rather than from a hand copy. Regenerate it after any catalogue change.

**Nothing here was fetched by this repository's tooling.** Its network
policy refuses outbound HTTPS to everything but the model provider. Where a
record says a page was read, somebody else read it and supplied the reading:
`docs/source-checks/` holds one file per reading, naming who, when and what
the page said. Everything else is what the repository was told earlier,
mostly relayed from search summaries.

Records whose source was read: **202 of 347**, across 31 products whose price was read from the merchant rather than relayed. Most recent reading: 2026-09-14.

A recorded URL is a claim about where a figure came from. It is not
verification that the figure is right or current, and a search summary is
not a visit. A reading is evidence of what a page said on a date, and
nothing more: none of these figures was measured by anybody here.

This is an evidence inventory, not a launch list. Nothing here decides
what to publish or what order to publish it in. A product with no amount
on record is publishable behind "Check current price", which is what
the site already does; what a partner needs is to know which is which.

Each product is reported on three independent dimensions:

- **Comparison facts.** Do the specifications a shopper compares on exist,
  carry a usable value, and name a source?
- **Price evidence.** Is there an amount on record, was it read from the
  merchant or relayed from a summary, and when was it written down? None of
  these figures was re-checked today. **Recorded is not verified.**
- **Image readiness.** Is there a real image, and is there evidence of the
  right to use it?

A source URL on a record says where a figure is claimed to have come from.
It is not verification that the figure is right, that it is current, or that
anybody read that page. Where a record says `secondhand`, nobody did.

Two things apply to all 20 and are not repeated in each entry:

1. **Every image is a procedural placeholder.** There are no image files in
   `public/`, no licence, no permission and nothing to show. A public image
   URL is not permission and is not treated as any.
2. **No affiliate programme is in place for this site.** That is the
   owner's own statement, and the site says it on `/disclosure`. It is a
   different fact from each offer record's `affiliate.status`, which is
   `unknown` on all 26: unrecorded, not checked and found to be none. Every
   link is an ordinary one to the maker or retailer, carrying
   `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` and no tracking parameter.
