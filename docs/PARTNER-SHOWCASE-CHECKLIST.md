# Partner showcase checklist

What a prospective affiliate partner would find on each of the 20 products,
and what is missing before this site is worth showing them. Generated by
`scripts/partner-checklist.ts`, so every figure comes from the catalogue
rather than from a hand copy. Regenerate it after any catalogue change.

**No manufacturer page was fetched to produce this.** The environment's
network policy blocks outbound HTTPS to anything but the model provider, so
every source line below is what the repository records, not what a page said
today. A recorded URL is a claim about where a figure came from; it is not
verification that the figure is still right, and a search summary is not a
visit. Where this file says a source was not fetched, that is a statement
about this repository's evidence, not about the product.

Three verdicts, computed, not judged:

- **Show it.** A confirmed price, no prototype specifications, every
  required specification present and sourced, and it holds its place in the
  ranking on its own merits.
- **Show it, with the gaps named.** A confirmed price and a place in the
  ranking it earned, carrying specifications that are prototype data, or
  whose source is unrecorded, or whose source states nothing. Every one of
  those is marked on the page, and a partner will still ask about them.
- **Hold it back.** No confirmed price, or below the completeness floor.
  Either one invites the question the site cannot answer.

Two things apply to all 20 and are not repeated in each entry:

1. **Every image is a procedural placeholder.** There are no image files in
   `public/`, no licence, no permission and nothing to show. A public image
   URL is not permission and is not treated as any.
2. **No affiliate relationship exists on any offer.** Every link is an
   ordinary link to the maker or retailer, carrying
   `rel="sponsored nofollow noopener"` and no tracking parameter.

## The shortest path to something worth showing

In order, and smaller is better: a partner shown six products whose every
figure survives a question will believe the seventh. A partner shown twenty,
eight of which cannot quote a price, will check all twenty.

1. **Confirm the eight prices, or drop those products from the showcase.**
   Six of the eight red-light panels have no confirmed price, which is the
   category a red-light brand would look at first. Cold plunges are the one
   category where every price is real; that is the category to lead with.
2. **Replace or remove the 24 prototype specification values.** Each one is
   marked on the page, which is honest and still reads as unfinished. A
   removed value costs a completeness point; an invented one costs the site.
3. **Read the pages already recorded.** 124 of 196 attributes and 13 of 26
   offer prices were relayed from search summaries rather than read from the
   maker's page. The URLs are on file. This is ordinary work, needs no
   agreement with anybody, and moves the largest number in this document.
4. **Get images, or design around not having them.** 24 of 24 are
   procedural. No partner conversation survives a catalogue with no product
   photography, and a photograph found on a public page is not a licence.
5. **Set `NEXT_PUBLIC_SITE_URL`, add robots and a sitemap** before anyone is
   sent a link, or the canonical URLs point at localhost.
