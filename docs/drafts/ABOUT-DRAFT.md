## What this site is

Wellness Compare puts red light panels, cold plunges and wellness drinks side
by side on the specifications people actually choose between, and shows where
each number came from.

Three categories, 20 products, 17 brands.

## How a product gets ranked

Every product is scored on the same criteria as the others in its category,
with weights published on the How We Choose page. Red light panels are scored
on coverage, irradiance, LED count, warranty and returns. Cold plunges on
whether a chiller is included, insulation, sanitation, warranty, minimum
temperature and the height it fits. Drinks on sugar, added sugar, calories and
whether a subscription is offered.

A product scores zero on anything the record cannot support. Not a guess, not
an average of similar products: zero. That is why some products score lower
here than elsewhere, and it is deliberate.

Badges follow from the scores. Best Overall is the highest score. Best Value
blends the score with price. Best Budget and Best Premium are the best scores
in a price tier, awarded only when at least two products with a real price sit
in that tier, and never to a product scoring zero. When a badge cannot be
awarded honestly, the page says which one and why instead of quietly showing
fewer picks.

## Where the numbers come from

Most are the manufacturer's own claims, and the site says so on every one. A
figure read from a maker's page on a date is recorded as exactly that: read, by
whom, on what date, from which URL. A figure relayed from a search summary is
recorded as relayed, and never described as read.

Nothing on this site has been measured by anybody here. Recorded is not
verified, and the site does not pretend otherwise.

When a source states a figure two ways that do not agree, both are kept, the
conflict is shown, and the figure is not used for ranking. When a source states
a bound rather than a number, the bound is shown in the source's own words:
"less than 1 g", not "1 g". When a figure cannot be shown to describe the
product on the page, it is kept visible and used for nothing.

`docs/source-checks/` in the repository holds one file per reading: what the
page said, what was recorded, what was deliberately left alone, and what the
reading did to the rankings.

## Prices

Where a price has been confirmed, it is shown with the date it was written
down. Where one has not, the site says "Check current price" and links to the
retailer rather than showing a number nobody quoted.

A price recorded for a different product, or for a configuration that has not
been matched to the product on the page, is not shown at all. One product
currently has no price on this site for that reason.

## How this site is paid

There is no affiliate programme in place. No link here earns a commission
today. If that changes, the disclosure page changes with it, and the ranking
code cannot see affiliate status either way: it is not an input, and a test
proves the rankings are identical when every offer's affiliate status is
flipped.

## What this site is not

It does not give medical advice. No product here is presented as treating,
preventing or curing anything. Manufacturers' health claims are not repeated as
fact, and several have been removed from product descriptions for that reason.

It is also not finished. The product images are placeholders, several records
still rest on search summaries rather than pages anybody has opened, and the
site says which is which rather than hiding it.
