# Product images: what is established, for six representative products

Two products per category, all six from brands that already carry a source
check. Compiled 2026-09-10.

**Nothing in this file is permission.** A public image is not a licence, and an
absence of terms is not consent. Where a column reads "not established", that
is the finding, not a gap to be filled by assumption.

## Nobody has asked any brand for anything

Every finding below is **published terms, read on a date**. None of it is a
response to a request, because no request has been made. Where a term does not
permit reuse, that is a document saying what it says, not a company saying no
to us. The distinction matters: an approach to a brand is still an open path,
and nothing here has closed it.

## What this container could and could not do

This repository's container cannot reach any of these sites. Every brand host
was tried on 2026-09-10 and every one was refused at the proxy:

```
curl: (56) CONNECT tunnel failed, response 403
```

`hoogahealth.com`, `mitoredlight.com`, `www.renutherapy.com`, `icebarrel.com`,
`drinklmnt.com`, `drinkag1.com`: six attempts, six identical refusals. The
proxy's own status endpoint reports `connect_rejected`, "gateway answered 403
to CONNECT (policy denial or upstream failure)".

So **nothing in this file was read here**. Every term recorded below was read
by Codex, on a stated date, and is attributed to the page it came from. No
press, media or affiliate resource page has been read for any brand, and none
is claimed to exist: whether a brand publishes one is still open.

## Where the six stand

**Three brands' terms have been read, and none of the three permits what this
site would need.**

- **Hooga**: no affirmative licence; section 2 requires express written
  permission before copying.
- **Mito Red Light**: section 6 grants a personal, non-commercial licence and
  restricts republication. A comparison site is a commercial context, and the
  page itself directs other uses to `info@mitoredlight.com`, which is a route
  and not a permission.
- **Ice Barrel**: section IV restricts copying and republication absent written
  authorization, and warns that some material may belong to third-party
  rightsholders, so even the brand may not be able to license everything on its
  own page. Section V permits ordinary linking with no implied endorsement,
  which is exactly what this site already does.

**Two are unknown.** Renu and LMNT terms reads were attempted on 2026-09-09 and
failed. That is not a finding in either direction and must not be read as one.
AG1 has not been attempted.

**One has a documented asset-identity fault.** Hooga's PRO1500 gallery carries
images labelled PRO4500, so even with permission the assets on that page could
not be used without establishing which product each one depicts.

The LMNT product source check separately records that the page has photographs,
that no right was established, and that no image was taken. That is a record of
our conduct, not of their terms.


## Open questions, six products, one shape

For each brand, the same four questions, none answered here:

1. Does the brand publish a press, media or affiliate asset page at all?
2. If so, what does it permit: editorial use, comparison use, resale contexts?
3. What does it require: attribution, unmodified assets, prior approval, an
   affiliate relationship?
4. Which asset depicts the exact product on our record, given that four of the
   six sit on pages selling several variants?

Question 4 is the one that survives even a permissive answer to 1 through 3,
and it is the one this repository is already equipped to be strict about: the
same rule that keeps a 1500X figure off a 1500+ record applies to a photograph
of it.

## Next steps, in order

1. **Read the remaining terms and any asset page**, the way every product
   reading has been done: with the URL, the date and the exact wording
   recorded. Hooga, Mito and Ice Barrel are done and none of the three permits
   commercial reuse. Renu and LMNT were attempted and failed. AG1 has not been
   attempted.
2. **Record the finding per brand in the catalogue**, not just in a document.
   The `license` field on an image exists and is unused; a brand-level note of
   "terms read on this date, reuse not permitted" is worth as much as a
   permissive one.
3. **Where terms permit reuse, tie the asset to the product** before using it:
   file name, page, variant, and what the page says it depicts. Four of these
   six sit on multi-variant pages.
4. **Where terms require permission, asking is still open.** Three sets of
   published terms do not permit reuse; none of those is a company declining a
   request, because no request has been made. Mito's page even names an address
   for other uses. Making an approach is the owner's to do, and nothing here
   proposes, prepares or drafts one.
5. **Consider the alternative that needs no permission**: photographs of
   products somebody owns. That is the only path in this list with no legal
   dependency on a third party, and it does not scale to 20 products.

## What this file does not do

No account was created, no form submitted, no message sent, no asset
downloaded, and no page fetched. Six connection attempts were made and all six
were refused; that refusal is the evidence recorded above.
