Supplier import: a demonstration

Runs from a file on your own disk. No server, no account, no database.

What this does, and what it will not do.

It reads a supplier CSV you choose, in this browser, and shows what a draft record would look like and what is wrong with it. It writes nothing: the catalogue is untouched, no file is uploaded, no address is contacted, and nothing here can publish anything.

The sample files under docs/import-demo/samples/ are invented. The suppliers, the products and every figure in them were made up for this demonstration. They are not real products and their numbers are not facts about anything.
