Both are worth a minute in a live demo, and neither appears above, because every screenshot was captured on a page as it first loads, with nothing selected.

How it fits your needs.
  • Pick any filter and every card grows a short section reporting, requirement by requirement, what Matches, what Does not match, and what Needs confirmation.
  • The third state is the point. A filter has to refuse anything it cannot settle, so a missing figure and a genuine mismatch both read as "no". A shopper deciding needs to tell them apart: one rules a product out, the other is something to check with the maker.
  • It is not a score or a percentage and it never reads the capability score. It reports the shopper's own selections back.
  • Products the filters cannot place are counted under the grid rather than vanishing, with a line saying they are neither a match nor a miss.
  • To see it: open /cold-plunge, press Under $2,000, and read any card. Then open the comparison, which carries the same requirements.
The assistant, which is a scripted stand-in.
  • No language model is connected. Replies come from a small deterministic script, and the panel says so in a banner every time it opens.
  • It reads a budget and a few phrases and nothing more. Every sentence a shopper reads is written by the site from its own catalogue, never by a model.
  • What it does well is refuse: a medical question gets a fixed redirect, an off-topic one gets an honest "this site has no vitamins catalogue" with links, and asking about cold plunges on the red-light page offers a link to that section rather than three red-light panels.
  • To see it: open /red-light, press Help me choose, and type a full body panel under $700.

Deliberately unfinished, and visible in the shots above

  • Every product image is a procedural placeholder, marked DEMO IMAGE on the picture itself. Nothing is presented as a photograph, and no brand's photography is used. Permission has not been asked for, from anyone.
  • Specs say how they were sourced. A figure read from the maker's page, one relayed by a search summary, and one relayed by a retailer's listing all read differently. Prototype values are tagged Demo data and are withheld from filtering and scoring.
  • One product has no price at all, on purpose, and its page says why. Another shows no way to buy because its maker states it has gone out of business.
  • Four records cannot yet name their own configuration. Where that is true the affected figure is marked disputed and withheld rather than guessed.

Before this goes anywhere

Two groups, in the order they block things. Nothing here has been sent, published or deployed.

Before asking brands for images
  • Sender name, role and reply address.
  • How a private demo is shown: attached screenshots, a screen share, or a link if one exists. A domain is not required.
  • Three emails are drafted and unsent, with verified recipients, in docs/drafts/IMAGE-PERMISSION-EMAILS.md. They ask for exact-model assets and say where the images would appear.
  • Terms already read are in docs/drafts/IMAGE-RIGHTS-MATRIX.md.
Before publishing
  • A domain, and the variable that turns indexing on. Unset, the site refuses indexing by design.
  • A host.
  • Identity and contact: the four blanks in PRIVACY-DATA-INVENTORY.md, and an address for a contact page.
  • A retention window, after which the deletion is ours to build.

The short version of where everything stands is docs/PARTNER-PREVIEW-READINESS.md. The four unresolved product identities, with the page to read for each, are in docs/LAUNCH-BLOCKING-CONFLICTS.md.
