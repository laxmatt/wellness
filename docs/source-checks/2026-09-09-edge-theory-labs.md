A merchant told Codex the brand is no longer in business. That is a claim by
one party with an interest in the answer, and nobody has read the
manufacturer's own site, a filing, or a support channel to check it.

## What was recorded

The claim, in the product's source note and in its offer note. Nothing else
moved:

- **availability stays `unknown`.** It was already unknown. Marking it
  discontinued would turn a merchant's sentence into a fact about a company.
- **The $5,490 offer stays.** It is still what the record holds, and it is
  still flagged as relayed rather than read. Removing it would assert the
  opposite claim, that the offer is dead, on no better evidence.
- **No score, badge or ranking changed.** The claim is not an attribute, so
  it cannot be. The tub is still fourth of six in Cold Plunges on 44.4.

## What this record still needs

The Edge entry was always the weakest in the category: every figure is a
search summary, four attributes are prototype demo values, and the price
carries a placeholder image. The closure claim adds one more question rather
than answering any of them. Before this product is shown to a partner as
buyable, somebody has to read the manufacturer's page, confirm the price and
the warranty, and confirm the company answers support at all.

If a reading finds the site down or the product gone, that is the point at
which availability changes, and the reading is what changes it, not this
claim.
