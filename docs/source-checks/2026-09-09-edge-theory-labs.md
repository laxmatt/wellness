| Manufacturer page | https://www.edgetheorylabs.com/products/the-edge-tub-elite, still unread |

A Shop merchant listing for this tub states the brand is no longer in business.
That is a third-party storefront's sentence about a company. It is not the
maker speaking, not a filing, and not a support channel, and nobody has read
the manufacturer's own site to check it.

## What was recorded

The claim, with its source, in the product's source note and in its offer note.
Nothing else moved:

- **availability stays `unknown`.** It was already unknown. Marking it
  discontinued would turn a storefront's sentence into a fact about a company.
- **The $5,490 offer stays.** It is still what the record holds, and it is
  still flagged as relayed rather than read. Removing it would assert the
  opposite claim, that the offer is gone, on no better evidence.
- **No score, badge or ranking changed.** The claim is not an attribute, so it
  cannot be. The tub is still fourth of six in Cold Plunges on 44.4.

## What this record still needs

The Edge entry was always the weakest in the category: every figure is a search
summary, four attributes are prototype demo values, and the image is a
placeholder. The listing adds one more question rather than answering any of
them. Before this product is shown to a partner as buyable, somebody has to
read the manufacturer's page, confirm the price and the warranty, and confirm
the company answers support at all.

If a reading finds the site down or the product gone, that is the point at
which availability changes, and the reading is what changes it, not this
listing.
