## What the page stated, consistently

$199. Wavelengths 660 nm and 850 nm. 60 LEDs. 80 W actual consumption, against
the 300 W in the product's own name, which is the LED rating rather than the
draw. 12.2 x 8.2 x 2.5 inches, 6 lb. A 3-year warranty. A 60-day return window
starting at delivery, with return shipping deducted from the refund.

All of it is the maker's claim about its own product. None of it was measured.

## What the page stated inconsistently

These are recorded as conflicts and left unresolved. Picking the passage that
suits the site is how a comparison site stops being one.

1. **Irradiance.** The highlights say **over 73 mW/cm² at 6 inches**. The
   specification table says **73** exactly. The record keeps the floor, which
   both readings support, and its note says the page disagrees with itself.
   The site already declines to rank a bounded figure, so the disagreement
   costs nothing and is not papered over.
2. **Coverage.** The page describes the panel both as one for targeted
   sessions and as half-body. Nothing maker-reported is taken from it. The
   value stays the editorial classification computed from panel height, at its
   existing tier, with the conflict recorded in its note.
3. **Stock.** The page showed a "Sold out" label and an "Add to cart" control
   at the same time. Availability stays `unknown`. An add-to-cart control was
   never evidence of stock, and here the page does not even agree with itself.
4. **Features.** Timer and handle passages differ between sections. No
   attribute on this record depends on either, so nothing is recorded from
   them.

## What changed in the catalogue

- The **direct Hooga offer** now carries $199.00, read from the page, replacing
  $139.00 that was prototype data with a note saying no price had been found.
- **wavelengths_nm, led_count, warranty_years, return_window_days,
  irradiance_distance_in, dimensions, weight, warranty, returnPolicy**: same
  values, moved from relayed to read, each note keeping its earlier history.
- **dimensions** gains a height of 2.5 inches, which the record did not carry.
- **returnPolicy** text now says the return shipping is deducted, which the
  page states and the record did not.
- **power_w = 80** is new. The record had no power figure at all.
- **irradiance_mw_cm2** keeps its value and its bound, with the conflict above
  written into its note.

Not touched: the Amazon offer, the images (they exist on the page; no right to
use them is established), and every marketing or health claim on the page.

## One consequence worth a decision

The site still shows "Check current price" for this panel. The price it shows
is the lowest offer, and the Amazon offer's $149.00 is prototype data that
happens to be lower than the real $199.00 now on file. A placeholder is
outranking a fact.

The fix is one rule: choose the lowest offer whose amount is real, and fall
back to the current behaviour only when every offer is prototype data. That
changes which price the site displays and therefore what the value formula
sees, so it is left for review rather than taken here.
