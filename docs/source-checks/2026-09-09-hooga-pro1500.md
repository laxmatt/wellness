## What the page stated, consistently

$1,199. Wavelengths 660 nm and 850 nm. 300 dual-chip LEDs. 530 W consumption,
against the 1500 in the product's name, which is an LED classification rather
than draw. 36 x 8.6 x 3.1 inches. 21 lb 2 oz as the technical weight, which the
page's own table rounds to 21 lb. A 3-year warranty and a 60-day trial. The
maker describes the panel as full body. A door mount, hanging kit and adjustable
pulley are included; the modular stand is compatible and mobile stands are sold
separately. There is a digital timer.

All of it is the maker's claim about its own product. None of it was measured.

## What the page stated inconsistently

**Irradiance.** The highlights say **over 189 mW/cm² at 6 inches**; the
technical table says **189** exactly, with no distance. Recorded as `disputed`:
the value stays visible as "189 mW/cm², disputed", both statements stay in its
note, and it answers no question, scores nothing and ranks nothing. No
measurement distance is recorded either, because the two statements do not
share one.

**Stock.** A "Sold out" label and an "Add to cart" control at the same time.
Availability stays `unknown`, and the offer note says why.

  3.1-inch depth.
- **weight** is new on this record: 21.125 lb, the page's 21 lb 2 oz, with its
  own table's rounding in the note.
- **power_w = 530** is new.
- **returnPolicy** reads "60-day trial", the page's own word, where it said
  "60-day return period".
- **coverage** keeps the value `full_body` and moves from an editorial class
  computed from panel height to the maker's own description, which agrees with

  `["door_hang", "floor_stand_optional"]`. The page includes the door mount,
  hanging kit and pulley, and says stands are sold separately. The old value
  claimed a stand outright.
- **irradiance_mw_cm2** keeps its value, loses its bound and is marked
  disputed.

Not touched: the Amazon offer, the images, and every health or marketing claim.
