## The warranty, and the number that was never stated

The record carried `warranty_years: 10`, unsourced. No source states ten years,
and the page states a limited lifetime warranty, which names no number at all.

Removing the ten leaves a required, scored field with nothing in it, and a
lifetime warranty scoring as "missing" would be perverse. This category already
caps warranty scoring at five years, so the record now says **more than five
years**, as a bound, with the reason in its note. That claims no figure of its
own: a lifetime term satisfies it, the cap means nothing above five scores
differently, and a shopper filtering for ten years gets nothing rather than a
made-up match. The warranty text is what the page shows, and it says lifetime.

The Ice Barrel 400 carries the same unsourced ten years and the same "Lifetime"
text. It is not touched here, because its page has not been read. It is the
first thing to check when it is.
