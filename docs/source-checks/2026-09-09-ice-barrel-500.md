## The warranty, corrected twice

The record carried `warranty_years: 10`, unsourced. No source states ten years,
and the page states a limited lifetime warranty, which names no number at all.

This file first replaced the ten with a bound: more than five years, five being
this catalogue's scoring cap. That was wrong, and it is corrected. "Limited
lifetime" does not establish a manufacturer guarantee longer than five years;
the five was this project's own scoring policy dressed as a claim about the
product, and a shopper filtering for "at least 5 years" would have matched on
it as though the maker had said so.

The numeric is withdrawn. `warranty_years` is `not_stated`, the note says the
page names no term, and the warranty text a shopper sees is the maker's own:
"Limited lifetime warranty". The five-year scoring cap is untouched; it is an
editorial policy about how warranties are scored, and it stays on the category
where such policies belong rather than inside a product record.

**What that costs.** Warranty is a scored, required field. Withdrawing the
number drops the 500's score from 38.9 to 22.2 and moves Best Value to The Cold
Pod. A lifetime warranty scoring as if unknown is unsatisfying, and it is
honest: the catalogue holds no number, and inventing one to score better is the
thing this project keeps refusing to do.

**The 400, the same.** Ice Barrel 400 carried the same unsourced ten years
against the same "Lifetime warranty" text, and its numeric is withdrawn for the
same reason. Its page could not be read: the product URL redirected to the
maker's home page when Codex tried it on 2026-09-09. A redirect says the URL no
longer serves that product page. It does not say the product is discontinued,
the link is dead, or the company has changed anything, and the record claims
none of those. Its offer note now carries the redirect and the distinction.

Its score falls to 0, which exposed a badge defect: with The Cold Pod holding
Best Value, the 400 was the only remaining budget-tier candidate and took Best
Budget at a score of zero. Fixed narrowly, in `assignBadges`: a tier badge now
requires a positive score as well as eligibility. Best Budget in Cold Plunges
is withheld, with the reason stated on the page. No badge stacking was added
and no score was tuned to hand the badge back to a previous winner.

**What is not being done.** A lifetime warranty could be mapped to a score by
editorial rule, the way the five-year cap already is. That is a scoring policy
decision, separate from this record, and it must never be written into the
product as a manufacturer figure. That is exactly the mistake this file
documents twice.

## What was not recorded

**Minimum temperature.** Without the optional chiller there is none, and the
record holds none.

**Plumbing.** The page describes 3/4 in NPT ports and a drain spout assembly.
The record's `plumbing` field classes power and plumbing requirements, and
those hardware details do not settle it, so it is unchanged and still
unsourced.

**Indoor placement, stock, images and health claims.** Nothing recorded, and no
image right is claimed or implied.

## The empty weight, two figures

The product page states 104 lb empty, read at 15:08 Pacific. The maker's home
page, read at 15:52 the same day, states 106 lb for the same model. Neither is
preferred: the weight carries `disputed`, the figure shown is the product
page's, and both are in the note. It is one field, not a reason to doubt the
rest of the record.
