Partial on purpose. The product page's web output exposed no numeric technical
specifications, so the figures that matter most to a comparison were not read
and are left exactly as they were: relayed, and marked relayed.

## What was recorded

- **warranty_years = 2**, unchanged in value, moved from relayed to read. The
  warranty page states a 2-year limited warranty for Gen 2 and Gen 3 modular
  devices bought by a consumer through joovv.com. Commercial purchases are
  excluded, and the note says so.
- **warranty text** now reads "2-year limited warranty, consumer purchases from
  joovv.com" rather than "2-year warranty". Both halves of that are on the
  page and both matter to somebody deciding.
- **return_window_days = 60**, unchanged in value, moved from relayed to read.
- **returnPolicy text** now reads "60 days from receipt, conditions apply", and
  its note carries the conditions the page states: the return must be
  postmarked inside the window, authorization is required, the device must be
  like new in its original packaging, the customer pays return shipping, the
  original shipping is not refunded, and final sale, closeout and open-box
  items are excluded.
- **app_control** and **pulsing**, both already true, moved from relayed to
  read: the product page states app controls and the Recovery+ pulsing mode.

## What was not recorded, and why

**The price.** The page shows $1,699, and which kit or configuration that
amount belongs to was not resolved. The offer keeps its prototype $1,499 and
the page keeps saying "Check current price". Replacing one unverified amount
with another, better-looking one is not an improvement: it would read as a
confirmed price for a configuration nobody has established.

**Every numeric technical specification.** Irradiance, LED count, wavelengths
and dimensions were not exposed by the reading. They stay as they are, relayed
from a search summary and marked as relayed. The irradiance keeps its floor of
100 from the earlier record and is not touched by this reading.

**The Joovv Go's terms.** The returns page also carries a 30-day window and a
$50 fee. Those belong to a different product and are not carried into this one.

**Stock, images and health claims.** Nothing recorded. No right to any image is
claimed or implied, and nothing was taken from a therapeutic claim.

## What it changed in the rankings

Nothing. The four values that moved were already the values on record; what
changed is that four of them now cite a page somebody read rather than a
summary somebody relayed, and two policy texts say what the policies actually
require. Joovv's score stays 69.2, it holds no badge, and its shown price is
still "Check current price".
