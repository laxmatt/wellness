## What the page stated

The standard USA model at **$119.99**, with a Spaceship Cover offered as a
promotion. **85 gallons.** 29.53 in across and 29.53 in high. 7.72 lb. Indoor
and outdoor. Insulated and portable. Supplied with a cover, six legs, a pump,
a drain hose and a bag.

## What this record holds

**88 gallons**, at **$139.99**, from an Amazon listing relayed on 2026-09-08.
29.5 in on every side. The same package: cover, pump, drain hose, bag,
insulated, six legs.

## Near match, not identity

The physical size is the same measurement, 29.53 in and 29.5 in being the same
number rounded differently. The package contents match item for item. The
capacity does not: 85 against 88. The price does not: $119.99 against $139.99.

Two readings fit those facts. One tub, described differently by its maker and
by a marketplace listing, in which case one of the capacities is wrong. Or two
products, in which case the maker's figures were never about this record.

Nothing here can tell them apart, and guessing would mean either quietly
changing a capacity or attaching a maker's price to a listing that never
carried it.

## What was done

- **The capacity is marked as unreliable.** Both figures and both sources are
  in its note. It stays visible on the page and is withheld from matching and
  scoring. It is not scored in this category, so nothing moved.
- **The 88 was not changed to 85**, and the record was not renamed.
- **No manufacturer price was created.** $119.99 is the maker's price for the
  85-gallon model. Putting it on an 88-gallon record would invent an offer.
  The record keeps its relayed $139.99, still labelled relayed.
- **Nothing from the maker's page was promoted onto this record's values.**
  Indoor and outdoor placement is still a placeholder here, and insulation is
  still relayed, because the page that states them is the page whose identity
  is in question.
- **Stock stays unknown.** The maker's page shows Immediate Dispatch, a Notify
  When Available control and cart behaviour that disagree with each other. A
  page arguing with itself about stock is not evidence of stock.

## What this reading does not support

The **maximum user height** on this record, 79 in, has no support from it. Nor
does any **warranty**: the record holds none and this reading supplies none.
No **image rights**, and no health claim.

## What would settle it

Reading the Amazon listing itself, which nothing here has ever done, or finding
a maker's page for an 88-gallon model. Until one of those happens, this record
is what it always was: a marketplace listing relayed once, now with a note
saying that the maker's own catalogue does not obviously contain it.
