## Joovv Solo 3.0: read, and the page answers a different question

Page read: https://joovv.com/blogs/joovv-blog/red-light-therapy-buyers-guide

The guide sets out how to choose between Joovv's mounting options, naming a
Boot Floor stand, a Door mount and a Nano Wall mount for the Solo, and a Mobile
Stand for the Max, Quad and Elite only.

That is a guide to what a buyer may pick, not a statement of what ships with the
unit this record prices. `mounting` is a list of what the maker says comes with
or is offered for this product, and the three named options are choices made at
purchase: which one our offer includes is exactly what the page does not say.

Recording all three would claim the panel arrives with a door mount, a floor
stand and a wall mount together. Recording one would be picking. The field stays
absent, and the record now says why: this is a configuration question, of the
same kind as Plunge's cold-only versus heater tubs, not a missing figure.

The Mobile Stand is explicitly for other models and is not transferable here.
