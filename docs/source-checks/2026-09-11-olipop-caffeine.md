## What the page stated

A section headed "Caffeine in OLIPOP" names the caffeinated flavours, as
Vintage Cola, Cherry Cola, Doctor Goodwin and Citrus Rush, and then states:

> The rest of our flavors are caffeine-free!

A cheat sheet on the same page repeats that the remaining flavours have zero
caffeine. Classic Root Beer appears on that page, in both the 9g and 6g fiber
navigation, and is not among the four named.

## What was recorded

**`caffeine_mg` = 0, `manufacturer_reported`**, cited to this page, read
direct on 2026-09-11. The field was `not_stated`.

## Why this is not the inference this project refuses

Silence is not a zero, and that rule has not moved. It is why AG1, Cure and
Liquid I.V. still hold no caffeine figure today, and why this record held none
until now.

What changed is not the strength of an absence but the arrival of a statement.
The earlier reading, on 2026-09-09, reached the product page, and that page
does not mention caffeine. A page that does not mention caffeine says nothing.
This page does mention it: it names the exceptions and then speaks about every
flavour that is not one of them. A sentence about the rest of the range is a
sentence about each member of the rest of the range, made once. Classic Root
Beer is in that set because the same page lists it and does not name it as an
exception.

The distinction to hold on to, because the next record will test it: an
exhaustive statement covers what it covers, and an absent mention covers
nothing. Demanding that a maker name every flavour individually would have
refused the best evidence this field has, on a page written specifically to
answer the question.

## What did not change

**The other three gaps.** AG1, Cure Hydration Lemonade and Liquid I.V.
Hydration Multiplier stay `not_stated`. Their FAQ pages were retrieved on
2026-09-11 and no caffeine text was exposed in them, which says nothing about
whether those pages state one; Codex is checking them separately. The exact
URLs and the question for each are in
`2026-09-11-caffeine-attempt-blocked.md`.

**LMNT Citrus Salt's zero**, which still rests on a 2026-09-08 search summary
while the one direct reading of its cited page deliberately left caffeine alone.
Unchanged and still the weakest record the caffeine filter acts on.

**Celsius's 200 mg**, still citing an Amazon listing as the manufacturer.

**Images.** Reading a page is not permission to use its pictures.

`caffeine_mg` is not a scoring criterion for this category, so a figure arriving
moves completeness and nothing else. No badge changed hands and no ranking
order moved.

In the fit section, OLIPOP goes from "Needs confirmation" to "Matches" against a
caffeine-free requirement, and from "Needs confirmation" to "Does not match"
against a caffeinated one. Both are now answers rather than shrugs, which is the
point of reading the page.

## A note on a name

A web search run from this container on 2026-09-11 named the fourth caffeinated
flavour differently. A search summary is not a reading and is not treated as a
conflict with one. It is written down here only so that a later reader who finds
a different name knows this was seen and set aside, rather than missed.

## A test rule that was wrong, and is gone

A rule added earlier that day forbade two products from citing the same page for
caffeine. It was aimed at the right worry, a figure moving between flavours, and
it was the wrong instrument: a maker's page about caffeine can state figures for
its whole range, and this one does. Uniqueness is not evidence quality, and the
rule would have refused this reading.

What replaced it asks the thing that actually matters. A record citing a page
shared with another product has to say in its note how that page reaches this
product.
